## Sources

- `bdb`: The Brown-Driver-Briggs Hebrew and English Lexicon by Francis Brown, S. R. Driver, and Charles A. Briggs (1906). Public domain. Entries are keyed to their Strong's numbers.
- `lsj`: A Greek-English Lexicon by Henry George Liddell, Robert Scott, Henry Stuart Jones, and Roderick McKenzie (1940). Public domain. Entries are keyed to their Strong's numbers.
- `thayer`: A Greek-English Lexicon of the New Testament by Joseph Henry Thayer (1889). Public domain. Entries are keyed to their Strong's numbers.
- `theodia`: Generated by Theodia. Each entry summarizes the senses, morphology, and usage of a Strong's number, with linked scripture references and cross-references to related words.
